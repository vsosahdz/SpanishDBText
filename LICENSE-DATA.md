The English side of every entry is reproduced from the source datasets and keeps
their original terms. The Spanish side was generated by the pipeline in this
repository.

## Source datasets

### WikiText-103

- **Source:** Merity, S., Xiong, C., Bradbury, J., & Socher, R. (2017).
  *Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models.* ICLR 2017.
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0 (originally CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL text from English
  Wikipedia, redistributed by Salesforce Research).
- **Used here:** 1,000 articles selected by stratified sampling from the
  29,423 articles of the `wiki.train.raw` split.

### FEVER (Wikipedia dump)

- **Source:** Thorne, J., Vlachos, A., Christodoulopoulos, C., & Mittal, A.
  (2018). *FEVER: a Large-scale Dataset for Fact Extraction and VERification.*
  NAACL-HLT 2018.
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0 (the underlying text is English Wikipedia).
- **Used here:** 10,000 passages selected by stratified sampling from a
  50,000-page shard of the FEVER Wikipedia dump (`wiki-pages`).

### Europarl

- **Source:** Koehn, P. (2005). *Europarl: A Parallel Corpus for Statistical
  Machine Translation.* MT Summit X. <https://www.statmt.org/europarl/>
- **Licence:** the proceedings of the European Parliament are published by the
  EU without copyright restrictions, and the Europarl release states that the
  corpus is free to use for research and commercial purposes.
- **Used here:** 1,006 debate files selected from 3,656 English chapter files
  (2009–2011 sessions). Only the English side was used; the official Spanish
  translations that ship with Europarl were deliberately left untouched so that
  they remain available as a human reference for future validation.

## What is not redistributed here

The full source datasets (about 7 GB expanded) are not included. Get them from
their original distributors with `scripts/download_datasets.py`. What `corpus/inputs/`
holds is only the selected subsets that the pipeline actually consumed, shipped
so that a run can be reproduced without downloading and re-sampling everything.
